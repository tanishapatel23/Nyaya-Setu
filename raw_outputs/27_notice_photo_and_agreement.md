Adv. Meera Joshi has sent you a legal notice on behalf of Mr. Prakash Kulkarni, demanding Rs 85,000 within 15 days. A notice is not a court case. It is usually the step before one, so it deserves a calm written reply before the deadline.

Time limit: Reply to the notice by 12 Nov 2026, 2 days from now.

Worth knowing
- If you paid the landlord a security deposit, your reply should mention it. Attach the rent agreement and I will pick out the amount.
- The notice claims repair or damage costs. Ask for the bills and photos behind that figure. Normal wear and tear is not damage.
- They threaten civil proceedings, meaning a case to recover money. That is not a criminal matter.

What to do
1. Today: Write down the date you got it and keep the envelope. Not replying at all is the worst option.
2. Before 12 Nov 2026: Send a written reply by registered post. A draft is in the Documents tab. Do not admit anything that is not true.
3. If needed: If it involves a cheque, property, the police or a court date, talk to a lawyer before replying. 15100 is free.

   [get]  your own records of the deal (agreements, receipts or chats that support your side)

The law, in plain words
[NOT-1] A legal notice is not a court case, but do not ignore it
    A legal notice is a formal letter, usually sent through a lawyer, saying what the other side wants and by when. It does not mean a case has been filed. But it is often the step before one, and if you stay silent they can later show the court that you never disputed it.
    Source: General practice
[NOT-2] Reply in writing, on the facts, before the deadline
    The usual response is a calm written reply, sent before the deadline by registered post, that answers their points one by one and gives your side. Do not admit things that are not true, and do not threaten them. If the notice is about a cheque, a property, or anything involving the police or a court date, get a lawyer to write the reply.
    Source: General practice
